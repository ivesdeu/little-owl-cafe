Find Your Purrfect Match

Every cat at The Little Owl has a story waiting to unfold with the right family. Our veterinary-led rescue approach ensures each feline friend is healthy, socialized, and ready to bring joy to their forever home. Browse our available cats and discover your new companion.

Your Adoption Journey

Three simple steps to finding your new best friend

1

Visit the Lounge

Enjoy a specialty beverage while observing our cats in their comfortable sanctuary. Get a feel for their personalities and see who catches your eye.

2

Meet the Cats

Spend quality time with cats that interest you. Our staff will share their stories, care needs, and help you find the perfect personality match.

3

Take One Home

Complete our streamlined adoption process and welcome your new family member home with all the supplies and support you need.

Our Adoption Process

We've designed our adoption process to be thorough yet welcoming, ensuring the best match for both you and your new feline friend.

  • Visit & Connect

    Spend time in our lounge meeting our available cats and finding your connection.

  • Application & Interview

    Complete our adoption application and have a brief conversation with our team.

  • Home Preparation

    We'll help you prepare your home and provide guidance on supplies and setup.

  • Welcome Home

    Take your new family member home with ongoing support from our team.

Adoption Fees

All cats come spayed/neutered, vaccinated, microchipped, and health-checked by our veterinary team.

Kittens (under 1 year): $150

Adult cats (1-7 years): $100

Senior cats (7+ years): $75
