Our Mission

Veterinary-led and woman-owned, The Little Owl represents a new approach to cat rescue and community building.

Founded by Christina Novak in 2026, we believe that healing happens in beautiful spaces where both cats and humans feel valued. Every cup of coffee sold supports our rescue efforts. Every class taught builds understanding. Every adoption celebrated creates ripples of compassion throughout Naperville and beyond.

We're not just a business—we're a movement toward intentional living, mindful community, and the belief that small acts of care can change everything.
